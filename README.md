One original English sentence, at most 200 characters, phrased as an absurd clause hidden deep inside a terms-of-service agreement — an entry for make.fun's The Absurd Clause Contest bounty ($2, top 3 paid).

## Entry

> By using this service you agree that your shadow becomes the property of the Company and may be leased to third parties during daylight hours.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) reads the line from `entry.txt` and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
